This repository (`ccea-sdk`) is licensed under **Apache-2.0** (see `LICENSE`).

## What is open vs proprietary

- **Open-source (this repo):** protocol contracts/schemas, cryptographic verification primitives, and guardrails intended to be auditable.
- **Proprietary (private repos):** trading/ML IP (training pipelines, simulators/backtests, execution models, strategies, integrations), commercial UI/enterprise features, and operational runbooks.

The high-level CCEA concept is public; competitive implementation details and operational playbooks remain proprietary.
